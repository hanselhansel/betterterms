# Rights

Each rule carries its source and the date it was read. Re-check rules
older than 90 days before relying on them. Negotiated business
contracts follow their own text first; consumer rules below matter
most when the user buys on a self-serve plan or pays by credit card.

## United States, federal: click-to-cancel status

Rule: The 8th Circuit struck down the FTC click-to-cancel rule on
2025-07-08. The FTC restarted rulemaking with an advance notice on
2026-03-11; comments closed 2026-04-13; no proposed rule as of
2026-10-03.

What it means: no federal click-to-cancel requirement is in force.
Cancellation of a self-serve API or cloud subscription follows the
contract and the vendor's posted policy, so read those terms before
planning an exit.

Source: https://www.cov.com/en/news-and-insights/insights/2026/03/ftc-launches-new-rulemaking-on-the-negative-option-rule (read 2026-10-03)

## United States, federal: credit card billing errors (Reg Z, 12 CFR 1026.13)

Rule: Credit cards: send a written billing error notice that the
issuer receives within 60 days after it sent the first statement
showing the error.

What it means: for API or cloud charges on a credit card, a billing
error dispute has a 60-day window from the statement date. Keep
statement dates in the case file; a dispute is an irreversible action
and always needs the user's explicit approval.

Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/13/ (read 2026-10-03)

## California: automatic renewals (AB 2863, Bus. and Prof. Code 17602)

Rule: From 2025-07-01: online sign-up means online cancel; online
flows allow one retention offer with cancel shown throughout; phone
agents must say you can cancel before making an offer; yearly
reminder; 7 to 30 days notice before a price change.

What it means: a California consumer on a self-serve subscription can
cancel online and must get advance notice of price changes. Business
contracts are outside this rule; check the user's intake answer on
personal versus company spend.

Source: https://www.dglaw.com/californias-enhanced-automatic-renewal-law-is-signed-into-law-key-changes-and-compliance-obligations/ (read 2026-10-03)

## California: bot disclosure (Bus. and Prof. Code 17941)

Rule: a person using a bot online may not mislead about its artificial
identity to incentivize a commercial transaction; the bot's identity
must be disclosed clearly when needed to avoid deception.

What it means: if the vendor sincerely asks whether an AI is
involved, never deny it. Draft the honest reply and hand it to the
user for approval.

Source: https://california.public.law/codes/business_and_professions_code_section_17941 (read 2026-10-03)
