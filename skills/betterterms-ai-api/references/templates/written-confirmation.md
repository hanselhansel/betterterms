# Template: request the terms in writing

Use when: the vendor described a concession in chat or on a call and
you need the contract language before any accept or sign action.

```text
Hello,

Thank you, we are close. Before we sign, please send the full terms
in writing: price, term length, renewal and notice dates, any
price-change clause, rate limits, and overage rates.

Once the document matches what we discussed, I can get it signed.

Best,
```

Notes:

- This is a `send` action, not an accept. The accept or sign draft
  comes later, after the user reads the written terms and gives an
  explicit yes.
- Add items the case needs (rollover, ramp schedule, data terms) to
  the list; drop items that do not apply.
