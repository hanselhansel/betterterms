# Template: opening renewal outreach

Use when: the renewal or contract end date is coming and no pricing
talk has started yet. Send to the account manager or renewals contact.
Written channels lose warmth, so keep the reason and the courtesy.

```text
Subject: Renewal pricing for our account

Hello,

Our renewal is coming up and we would like to settle pricing and terms
early this time, so both sides have room to plan.

We are reviewing our usage and our options for the next term. Could
you share renewal pricing and any programs that fit our usage profile?
Happy to share our forecast if that helps you shape an offer.

Thank you,
```

Notes:

- No prices in this message. The goal is to open the talk early and
  let them move first on numbers.
- If the user already has a written quote, skip this template and use
  `counter-anchor.md` or `counter-options.md`.
