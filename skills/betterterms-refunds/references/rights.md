# Refund rights

Rules this pack cites, each with a source and the date it was read.
Re-check anything older than 90 days before relying on it (research
skill rule). This is case information, not legal advice. When the user's
state or country is not listed, research the local rule fresh and record a
source record.

## United States, federal

| Rule | What it says | Source | Read |
|---|---|---|---|
| Credit cards, billing errors (Reg Z, 12 CFR 1026.13) | Send a written billing error notice that the issuer receives within 60 days after it sent the first statement showing the error. | https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/13/ | 2026-10-03 |
| Subscription cancellation, federal | The 8th Circuit struck down the FTC click-to-cancel rule on 2025-07-08. The FTC restarted rulemaking with an advance notice on 2026-03-11; comments closed 2026-04-13; no proposed rule as of 2026-10-03. | https://www.cov.com/en/news-and-insights/insights/2026/03/ftc-launches-new-rulemaking-on-the-negative-option-rule | 2026-10-03 |
| Negative-option billing (ROSCA) | ROSCA still applies to online negative-option plans while no federal click-to-cancel rule is in force. | https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2026/05/ftc-revives-clicktocancel-rule-new-risks-for-subscription-businesses | 2026-10-03 |

## California

| Rule | What it says | Source | Read |
|---|---|---|---|
| Automatic renewals (AB 2863, Bus. and Prof. Code 17602) | From 2025-07-01: online sign-up means online cancel; online flows allow one retention offer with cancel shown throughout; phone agents must say you can cancel before making an offer; yearly reminder; 7 to 30 days notice before a price change. | https://www.dglaw.com/californias-enhanced-automatic-renewal-law-is-signed-into-law-key-changes-and-compliance-obligations/ | 2026-10-03 |

The California row is a law-firm summary; confirm details against the
statute text (Bus. and Prof. Code 17602) before relying on them. It
matters for refunds when the disputed charge followed a cancellation
attempt.

## Merchant terms (contract, not law)

Some merchants restrict or close accounts after a chargeback. Read their
terms first and try the merchant's own refund process before disputing.
Source: https://store.steampowered.com/subscriber_agreement/ (a general
breach clause only; no official anti-dispute policy found), read
2026-10-03. Never name a merchant as banning disputes.

## Gaps to check before promising a window

- The 60-day rule above covers US credit cards. Debit cards, bank
  transfers, wallets, and other countries have their own rules;
  research the one that matches the user's payment method.
- Card networks and marketplace protection programs set their own case
  deadlines. Find the current figure from the official page during
  research and record it as a source record; do not rely on remembered
  numbers.
