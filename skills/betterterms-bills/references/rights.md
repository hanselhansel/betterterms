# Rights and rules by jurisdiction

Rules the user can lean on in a bills case. Each entry names the
jurisdiction, the rule, a source, and the date it was read. Confirm
the user's state or country at intake, and re-check any entry older
than 90 days before relying on it.

## United States (federal)

- Automatic renewal and cancellation: there is no federal
  click-to-cancel rule in force. The 8th Circuit struck down the FTC
  click-to-cancel rule on 2025-07-08. The FTC restarted rulemaking
  with an advance notice on 2026-03-11; comments closed 2026-04-13;
  no proposed rule as of 2026-10-03.
  (source: https://www.cov.com/en/news-and-insights/insights/2026/03/ftc-launches-new-rulemaking-on-the-negative-option-rule, read 2026-10-03)
- Until a new rule lands, the Restore Online Shoppers' Confidence Act
  (ROSCA) still applies to online subscriptions: clear disclosure,
  express consent, and a simple way to stop recurring charges.
  (source: https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2026/05/ftc-revives-clicktocancel-rule-new-risks-for-subscription-businesses, read 2026-10-03)
- Billing errors on a credit card (Regulation Z, 12 CFR 1026.13):
  send a written billing error notice that the issuer receives within
  60 days after it sent the first statement showing the error.
  (source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/13/, read 2026-10-03)

## California

- Automatic renewals (AB 2863, Business and Professions Code 17602),
  from 2025-07-01: online sign-up means online cancel; online flows
  allow one retention offer with cancel shown throughout; phone
  agents must say you can cancel before making an offer; yearly
  reminder; 7 to 30 days notice before a price change.
  (source: https://www.dglaw.com/californias-enhanced-automatic-renewal-law-is-signed-into-law-key-changes-and-compliance-obligations/, law-firm summary; statute text is Bus. & Prof. Code 17602, read 2026-10-03)

## How to use

- Rights back the ask in writing; they are not a threat. Quote the
  rule, ask for the outcome, stay courteous.
- A price-change notice period matters at promo end: in California,
  the provider owes the user 7 to 30 days of notice before the new
  price, which is itself an opening to negotiate.
- Cancellation rights set the exit floor. Where the user can cancel
  online or in the same medium as sign-up, the switch-or-stay
  decision is cheap to execute, which strengthens every ask.
- If the user's jurisdiction is not listed here, run a rights check
  as a research step before the plan: search the renewal, billing,
  and consumer-protection rules that apply, and log each as a source
  record.
