# Last ask before the exit path

One honest final ask when the patience budget is nearly spent. Never
request cancellation through a `send` draft; cancelling is the
`cancel` action and always needs an explicit yes.

Draft fields:

- `action`: `send`
- `offer`: the top of the ladder
- `period`: `month`
- `claims`: empty

```text
I appreciate your time on this. The offers so far are still more
than I can pay, so I am getting ready to move my service.

If you can do {offer}, I will stay. Otherwise I will look at
switching when my term allows. Thanks either way.
```
