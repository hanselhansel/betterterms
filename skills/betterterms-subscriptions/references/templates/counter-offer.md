# Counter-offer

When to use: answering an offer that is inside the band but above the
target. `{quote:1}` references their stated number from the inbound
`amounts` list; `{offer}` renders this draft's offer field with its
period; `{ladder:2}` is the next concession step. Pick one money move
per message, never all three. Every placeholder must resolve or the
gate blocks the draft.

```text
Thank you, I appreciate the offer. {quote:1} helps, but it is still
above what fits my budget. Could we meet at {offer}? If that is not
possible, {option:lower-tier} would also keep me. I am glad to decide
today if we can land near there.
```
