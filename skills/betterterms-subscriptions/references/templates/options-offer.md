# Options offer

When to use: presenting two or three equal ways to stay (MESOs) drawn
from the plan's options, such as annual billing, a lower tier, or a
pause. Keep only the `{option:<label>}` placeholders that exist in
`plan.yaml` and name real option labels. Every placeholder must resolve
or the gate blocks the draft.

```text
Thank you for checking. I would like to stay, and any of these would
fit: {option:annual}, {option:lower-tier}, or {option:pause}. Would one
of those be possible on your side?
```
