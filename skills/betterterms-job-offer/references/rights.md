# Rights

Each rule carries its source and the date it was read. Re-check rules
older than 90 days before relying on them. These shape what the user
may be asked, what must appear in a posting, and whether a noncompete
limits real alternatives.

## United States, federal: noncompete rule status

Rule: the FTC's Non-Compete Clause Rule was vacated by a federal
district court. On 2025-09-05 the FTC announced it had moved to
dismiss its appeals and accede to the vacatur. As of 2026-10-03 no
federal noncompete ban is in force; state law governs.

What it means: a noncompete clause in the current or new paperwork
is judged under the user's state law. Escalate it to the user when
it appears.

Source: https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/09/federal-trade-commission-files-accede-vacatur-non-compete-clause-rule (read 2026-10-03)

## California: salary history and pay scale (Labor Code 432.3)

Rule: an employer may not seek or rely on an applicant's salary
history; on reasonable request it must give the applicant the pay
scale for the position; and an employer with 15 or more employees
must include the pay scale in job postings.

What it means: a California user does not have to answer "what do
you make now" and can ask for the posted range. When the recruiter
probes for current pay, redirect to the range or the target.

Source: https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=LAB&sectionNum=432.3 (read 2026-10-03)
See also the Labor Commissioner page: https://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/california_equal_pay_act.htm (read 2026-10-03)

## California: noncompetes are void (Bus. and Prof. Code 16600)

Rule: except for narrow statutory exceptions, a contract restraining
a person from a lawful profession, trade, or business is void. The
statute voids employment noncompetes no matter how narrowly
tailored.

What it means: a California user's outside options are not limited
by a current employer's noncompete clause. In other states the same
clause may be enforceable; check before treating "I cannot leave" as
true.

Source: https://california.public.law/codes/business_and_professions_code_section_16600 (secondary mirror of the statute, read 2026-10-03; primary text: https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=BPC&sectionNum=16600.)

## Elsewhere

- Salary-history bans, posted-range requirements, and noncompete
  enforceability vary by state and city. During research, check the
  rules where the user will work and where the employer sits,
  and record each source with the date it was read.
- When the job is performed in one state and the employer sits in
  another, confirm which rules apply before relying on one.
