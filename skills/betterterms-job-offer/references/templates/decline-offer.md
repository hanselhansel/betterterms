# Template: decline and keep the door open

Use when: the user has decided to decline. Short, warm, and final;
no negotiation detail needed. Declining is the user's decision and
needs an explicit yes before any draft.

Draft fields:

- `action`: `send`
- `offer`: null
- `claims`: empty

```text
Subject: Re: offer for <role>

Hi <name>,

Thank you for the offer and for the time through the process.
After careful thought, I have decided not to move forward.

I have a lot of respect for the team, and I hope our paths cross
again. Thank you for understanding.

Best,
```

Notes:

- Keep it short. A long decline invites renegotiation the user did
  not ask for.
