# Confirm milestones after a no

Send after a no or a deferral: restate the milestones the manager
named and the dated check-in, so the roadmap survives memory and
staff changes. Playbook move 11.

Draft fields:

- `action`: `send`
- `offer`: null
- `period`: `once`
- `claims`: empty

```text
Subject: Next steps from our conversation

Hi <name>,

Thank you again for the direct answer today. To make sure I have
it right: the path to <the raise or the level> is <the milestones
they named>, and we will check in again on <the date they gave>.

I will put my plan against those milestones and keep the results
coming. Looking forward to picking this up then.

Best,
```

Notes:

- Fill the milestones from what the manager actually said in the
  debrief, never from the user's wish list. A milestone the manager
  did not name is a new ask, not a recap.
- Keep it to one short paragraph; the email's job is a dated
  record, not a second negotiation.
