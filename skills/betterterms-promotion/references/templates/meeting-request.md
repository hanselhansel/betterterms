# Meeting request

Ask for a dedicated meeting about role and pay, instead of raising
the topic inside a standing one on one. Send before the conversation,
once the numbers are fixed. No money in this message.

Draft fields:

- `action`: `send`
- `offer`: null
- `period`: `once`
- `claims`: empty

```text
Hi <name>,

I would like to schedule time to talk about my role and
compensation, and how I keep growing here. Could we book a
dedicated slot in the next couple of weeks rather than fold it
into our regular catch-up?

Happy to share the outline ahead of time if that helps.

Best,
```

Notes:

- Keep the ask to the meeting itself. The numbers stay in the
  script until the conversation.
- Send through `bt.py gate`; the `rendered` text is what the user
  sends.
