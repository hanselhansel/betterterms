# Rights

Rules that apply to cancelling a subscription, membership, service,
or contract. Each entry names its source and the date it was read.
Re-check anything older than 90 days before relying on it.

## United States, federal

- The 8th Circuit struck down the FTC click-to-cancel rule on
  2025-07-08. The FTC restarted rulemaking with an advance notice on
  2026-03-11; comments closed 2026-04-13; no proposed rule as of
  2026-10-03.
  Source: https://www.cov.com/en/news-and-insights/insights/2026/03/ftc-launches-new-rulemaking-on-the-negative-option-rule
  (read 2026-10-03)
- The Restore Online Shoppers' Confidence Act still applies to
  online subscriptions with recurring charges.
  Source: https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2026/05/ftc-revives-clicktocancel-rule-new-risks-for-subscription-businesses
  (read 2026-10-03)

## California

- From 2025-07-01: online sign-up means online cancel; online flows
  allow one retention offer with cancel shown throughout; phone
  agents must say you can cancel before making an offer; yearly
  reminder; 7 to 30 days notice before a price change. (AB 2863,
  Cal. Bus. and Prof. Code 17602, per a law-firm summary.)
  Source: https://www.dglaw.com/californias-enhanced-automatic-renewal-law-is-signed-into-law-key-changes-and-compliance-obligations/
  (read 2026-10-03)
  Statute text:
  https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billTextClient.xhtml?bill_id=202320240AB2863
  (read 2026-10-03)

## United States, credit cards

- Credit cards: send a written billing error notice that the issuer
  receives within 60 days after it sent the first statement showing
  the error. (Regulation Z, 12 CFR 1026.13; applies when a charge
  posts after a confirmed cancellation.)
  Source: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/13/
  (read 2026-10-03)

## Elsewhere

- Outside the rows above, the provider's own written terms govern.
  Record the user's state or country at intake and research the
  local rule before quoting rights.
